The Service In Detail

Diagnosis, repair, installation & maintenance. The full system, not just the symptom.

Every boiler call starts with a proper diagnosis. We don't reset the error code and hope. We inspect the whole heating system: the boiler itself, circulation pumps, zone valves, expansion tanks, pressure, venting, and water quality. Most "boiler problems" turn out to be system problems, and finding the real cause is the difference between a fix that lasts and a repeat service call. Once we know what's going on, we walk you through what we found and what your options are, with firm pricing before any work begins.

On the repair side, we handle everything from no-heat emergencies, short-cycling, and pressure loss to leaking components, kettling noise, and failed pumps, on all major boiler brands. We service residential boilers, in-floor heating systems, and the large hot water systems that serve condo and apartment buildings, where we plan our work around residents so nobody is left without heat or hot water.

When a boiler truly is at the end of its life, we'll show you the math and let you decide. As an authorized NTI dealer, we install high-efficiency systems sized correctly for your home or building. Sizing matters more than brand, and an oversized boiler wastes money for its entire lifespan. Every installation is done to manufacturer specification, which keeps your warranty fully valid.

Finally, we offer annual maintenance plans for homeowners and property managers alike: a full-system service that protects efficiency, extends equipment life, keeps warranties intact, and catches small issues before they become February emergencies. It's the least glamorous thing we do, and quietly the most valuable.

Common Questions

What customers ask us about boilers.

"My boiler keeps shutting off. Do I need a whole new one?"+
Not necessarily. Most shutdowns come down to a fixable component, pressure, or sensor issue. We'll diagnose it honestly and only recommend replacement when the math genuinely favours it.
"How often should I have my boiler serviced?"+
Once a year, ideally before heating season. Annual service keeps efficiency up, catches small problems early, and keeps most manufacturer warranties valid.
"How much does a new boiler cost?"+
It depends on the size of your home or building and the system you choose, but you'll always get a firm, upfront price before any work starts, with options at more than one price point.
