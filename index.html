Good to Know

Questions we hear a lot.

My hot water / in-floor heating stopped working. What should I do first?+
Check that your thermostat has power and your gas is on. If both look fine, don't start taking things apart. Boiler systems are complex and easy to make worse. Give us a call and we'll walk you through what to check safely before we even book a visit.
Do you really not pressure-sell? What's the catch?+
No catch. We'll lay out your options with real prices, tell you what we'd do in your shoes, and let you decide. If you want to shop around, we'll even suggest other reputable plumbers to get quotes from. We win on trust, not tactics.
How do your arrival windows work?+
We book service calls in 2-hour arrival windows rather than exact times. That way our plumbers never have to rush the job before yours, and yours gets the same respect. We'll text you when we're on the way.
Do you work with condo boards and property managers?+
Yes, it's one of our specialties. We service and install boiler and hot water systems for multi-unit buildings across Airdrie and Calgary, and boiler manufacturer reps regularly recommend us for this exact work. Ask about maintenance plans.
Are you available for emergencies?+
24/7. Burst pipes, floods, and no-heat calls in an Alberta winter can't wait until Monday. Call 403-899-9687 any time and you'll reach a real person.
