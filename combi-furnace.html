The Service In Detail

One compact unit. Your heat and hot water, handled.

A combi (combination) unit does two jobs at once: it heats your home and produces domestic hot water on demand, with no storage tank. When you open a hot tap, the unit fires instantly and heats water as it flows, which means no tank slowly leaking heat in your basement, no running out of hot water, and a footprint small enough to hang on a wall. For many homes, townhouses, and condos, it's the most efficient and space-smart way to do both jobs.

On the installation side, everything starts with a proper heat-loss and hot-water demand assessment. We calculate what your home actually needs, recommend a correctly-sized high-efficiency unit, and install it to manufacturer specification. Venting, condensate, gas supply, and water treatment are all done right, which protects both your safety and your warranty. We handle the removal of your old furnace and tank, and we leave the mechanical room cleaner than we found it.

For repairs, we work on all major combi brands. Common issues we resolve include pressure loss, error codes and lockouts, fluctuating hot water temperature, kettling noises, and failed diverter valves or sensors. Hard water scale is a frequent culprit in our region, so we check water quality as part of every diagnosis, treating the cause, not just the symptom.

Because a combi is doing double duty year-round, annual maintenance matters even more than with a conventional system. Our maintenance service includes a full inspection, cleaning, pressure and combustion checks, and descaling where needed, keeping efficiency high, bills low, and your manufacturer warranty fully intact. Ask us about maintenance plans when we quote your repair or installation.

Common Questions

What customers ask us about combi systems.

"What exactly is a combi furnace, and how is it different from what I have now?"+
It's a single high-efficiency unit that heats your home and makes hot water on demand, replacing both your furnace/boiler and your hot water tank in one compact, wall-hung appliance.
"Will a combi keep up with my family's hot water use?"+
Yes, when it's sized correctly, which is exactly why we assess your real usage before recommending a unit. An honest sizing calculation matters more than any brand name.
"Is a combi worth it for a smaller home or condo?"+
Often it's the best possible fit. You reclaim the floor space a tank was using, cut standby heat loss, and get hot water that never runs out.
