The Service In Detail

If water runs through it, we repair it.

Our repair work covers the full plumbing system, supply to drain. On the fixture side, that means dripping faucets, running toilets, leaking shower valves, garburators, and fixture replacements. These are fast, tidy jobs that stop the slow waste of water and money. On the supply side, we repair leaking and burst pipes, replace failing shut-off valves, and fix low water pressure at its source. Alberta winters are hard on plumbing, so frozen pipe thawing, repair, and prevention are a big part of our winter work. If a cold snap is coming, we can help you protect vulnerable lines before they burst.

For drains, we clear slow sinks, clogged tubs, and recurring backups, and we'll tell you honestly whether you're dealing with a one-time clog or a symptom of something bigger, like a bellied or root-invaded line. Where the cause isn't obvious, we use proper leak detection to find the real source of water damage before cutting a single hole. The goal is always the smallest repair that genuinely solves the problem.

When it's an emergency, like a burst pipe, a flooding fixture, or sewage backing up, call us at any hour. A real person answers, walks you through shutting off the water safely, and dispatches a plumber promptly. Emergency or not, our process is the same: diagnose thoroughly, explain your options with firm pricing up front, fix it right, and clean up after ourselves.

Every repair also comes with honest prevention advice. If we see something that will cause trouble later, like a corroding valve, a tank near end-of-life, or a vulnerable pipe run, we'll point it out and let you decide. No scare tactics, no upselling. Just the information you need to take care of your home.

Common Questions

What customers ask us about repairs.

"Do you charge extra for evenings and weekends?"+
After-hours emergency calls do carry a different rate, but we tell you exactly what it is before we dispatch. You'll never discover it on the invoice.
"My faucet only drips a little. Is it even worth a call?"+
A small drip wastes thousands of litres a year and usually signals wear that gets more expensive to fix later. We'll sort it quickly, or tell you honestly if it can safely wait.
"A pipe just burst. What do I do right now?"+
Shut off your main water valve (usually in the basement near the water meter) and call us at 403-899-9687. We answer 24/7 and dispatch immediately for active leaks.
