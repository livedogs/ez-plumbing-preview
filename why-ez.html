Our Mission

Honest advice. Quality workmanship. Fair price.

The trades have a trust problem, and we understand why. Too many companies belittle their competitors, pressure homeowners into work they don't need, and treat every service call as a sales opportunity. Every time that happens, it plants a seed of mistrust, not just in one company, but in all of us.

EZ Plumbing & Heating was built to be the opposite of that. We over-educate our clients: we show you what we found, explain your options in plain language, and give you the information to make the best decision for your home or property, even when the best decision is the one that makes us less money. You'll never hear us bad-mouth another plumber, and if you want a second opinion, we'll give you names of other good companies to call.

And when we get something wrong, because after thousands of jobs sometimes we do, we own it and make it right. No excuses, no runaround. Taking it on the chin is part of the job description here. That's what we mean by plumbers of character.
